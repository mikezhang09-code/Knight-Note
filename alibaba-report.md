## 1. Business Summary / 业务概要

Alibaba Group is the world's largest e-commerce company by GMV, operating China's dominant consumer-to-consumer (Taobao) and business-to-consumer (Tmall) marketplaces. Revenue comes from six segments: (1) **Taobao & Tmall Group** — the core cash engine via customer management (advertising) and commissions; (2) **Cloud Intelligence Group** — Alibaba Cloud, China's #1 public cloud provider and the AI pivot centerpiece; (3) **International Digital Commerce** — AliExpress, Lazada, Trendyol; (4) **Cainiao** — logistics network; (5) **Local Services** — rebranded "Taobao Instant Commerce" (formerly Ele.me); and (6) **Digital Media & Entertainment**.

The company is in the midst of a strategic transformation — CEO Eddie Wu's "user first, AI-driven" strategy is redirecting profits and free cash flow into (a) AI cloud infrastructure (RMB 120B+ capex over 4 quarters), (b) quick commerce expansion, and (c) the Qwen AI ecosystem which has surpassed 300 million monthly active users.

### Key Risks

1. **VIE Structure Risk / 可变利益实体风险**: ADR holders own Cayman entity, not operating company. Chinese government could void VIE contracts. HK secondary listing (9988) partially mitigates but doesn't eliminate.

2. **Regulatory & Geopolitical Risk**: "Common prosperity" overhang continues. PCAOB audit access resolved but HFCAA delisting risk persists as a tail scenario. US-China tech decoupling could restrict cloud/AI exports.

3. **Competitive Erosion in Core Commerce**: PDD Holdings (Pinduoduo/Temu) growing faster; Douyin (TikTok's parent) entering search-based e-commerce; JD competing aggressively in instant delivery. Alibaba's domestic GMV market share declining.

4. **Massive AI Capex Cycle — Earnings Suppression**: RMB 120B+ in AI/cloud capex over 4 quarters. FY2025 FCF down 53% YoY. Q3 FY2026 FCF collapsed 71% YoY. Profitability will remain under pressure through FY2027–28.

5. **Quick Commerce Cash Burn**: Taobao Instant Commerce (formerly Ele.me) not expected to generate positive cash flow until FY2028. Continued subsidy wars with Meituan.

6. **Earnings Quality**: GAAP earnings volatile due to mark-to-market swings on equity investments (Ant Financial, etc.). Non-GAAP adjustments are significant.

7. **Management Transition**: Post-Jack Ma era; CEO Eddie Wu executing AI pivot but track record still being proven. Capital allocation shifting from buybacks to heavy reinvestment.

## 6. Verdict & Positioning / 投资结论

### Rating: HOLD / WATCHLIST ⏸️
### 评级: 持有 / 观察名单

**Business Quality**: Alibaba remains a narrow-moat business with genuine competitive advantages in marketplace network effects, cloud computing (#1 in China), and the emerging AI ecosystem (Qwen). However, competitive pressure from PDD and Douyin is real, and the core commerce moat is narrower than it was 3–5 years ago.

**Valuation**: At $131, the stock trades at roughly fair value on our base-case DCF ($130). The P/E of 17.8× is below Alibaba's 10-year historical average of ~32× and below US tech peers, but appropriate given China risk discount. When applying the required 15% VIE/ADR margin of safety adjustment, the stock is above our adjusted entry target of $111.

**Key Thesis**: The investment case is binary around the AI pivot. If Alibaba Cloud's 36% revenue growth and AI monetization trajectory continues (10 consecutive quarters of triple-digit AI revenue growth), the bull case ($164+ ADR-adjusted) becomes achievable. If the AI capex cycle doesn't translate to durable competitive advantage and the core commerce continues losing share, the bear case ($77–91) is plausible.
